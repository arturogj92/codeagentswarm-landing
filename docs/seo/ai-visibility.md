## Acquisition semantics

Sources currently recognized: ChatGPT (including chat.openai.com), Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Copilot. Match exact referrer domains or exact decoded `utm_source` values. A lookalike domain is not a match. If two recognized AI sources disagree, the explicit UTM source wins.

The report discovers actual referrers/query strings from Umami and fetches exact filtered events. It deduplicates by event ID and session ID, so a pageview with both a ChatGPT referrer and a ChatGPT UTM counts once. It paginates fully, validates dates and filter results and fails on incomplete, changing or excessive results. Event requests use at most seven days per query, bounded pages and short-lived token renewal because broad monthly scans and deep OFFSET pagination on this instance are slow.

A download click belongs to the last observed AI pageview before that click in the same Umami session and requested window. This is last **observed AI** attribution, not full marketing last-touch attribution: later non-AI touches are not read, cross-device identity is unavailable and a visit before the window cannot earn credit. Session IDs are Umami identifiers, not guaranteed unique people. One session may visit from more than one AI; do not add the provider session counts. Home, guide and guide-index clicks remain separate. Clicks do not prove installations. Raw identifiers stay in memory; output is aggregate only.
